# Code of Conduct

Padcode follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@thinxpro](https://github.com/thinxpro), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict anyone who violates this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
